## Net effect on headline numbers

- 2020→2024 change unchanged: **$198.7B → $288.7B = +$90.0B (+45.3%)**.
- Patients per drug: **+$60.6B** (was +$61.7B) — now restricted to brands with a trustworthy patient count.
- Utilization: **+$4.0B** (was +$4.4B) — per-patient claims/units only.
- **New `claims_fallback` bucket: +$1.6B** — total-claims growth on the 188 brands without a usable patient count.
- Price (gross spend per dosage unit), new drugs, survivor-correction steps, negotiation and concentration figures are unchanged.
